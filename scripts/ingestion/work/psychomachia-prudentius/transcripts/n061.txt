Each tent was struck; each canvas open lay'd,
That none might slumber in the darksome shade.
The *pow'rs* assembled with impatience wait,
Anxious to know the bus'ness of the state,
Why, when no more the marks of war were seen,
CONCORD decreed a council shou'd convene,
What for the publick good FAITH meant to do,
Amend *old* edicts, or promulgate *new*.
Then CONCORD first the gen'ral silence broke,
Drew deep attention, and serenely spoke.
*Thrice happy Virtues*! now compleatly blest!
Henceforth consign'd to everlasting rest!
Still true to God, and to the christian name!
Full is th' allotted measure of your fame:
Extinct the foe, that would your peace confound,
And beat your *sacred city* to the ground;
With hostile flames the *holy place* would burn,
And all its glories into ashes turn.
Now *private friendship* labour to improve,
For *publick peace* is built on *private love*;
