By thee seduc'd, unwary souls begin
To walk unthoughtful in the ways of sin;
Blindly they enter, by thy soothings led,
Soon grow defil'd, and sink among the dead.
Hence then to hell, thou *fury*, speed thy flight,
And howl in dungeons of eternal night;
Or stretch'd at length on some sulphureous stream,
Or roll'd in whirling vortices of flame:
On earth no more uplift thy baleful face,
To tempt the *heroes* of the *christian race*;
O'er them in quiet let MESSIAH reign,
And keep his flock unconscious of a stain.
Thus spake the maid; and joyful to foresee
The growing blessings of her victory,
She seeks the silver stream of JORDAN's flood,
To *wash* the steel, and wipe away the blood,
That of the clotted gore no foul remain
In time the splendor of the sword shou'd stain;
Then walks triumphant to the fair abode,
And *bloodless altar* of the christian God;
