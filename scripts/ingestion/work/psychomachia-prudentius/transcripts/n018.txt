She puts all hope of conquest on her sword,
The only refuge now that arms afford.
Pois'd from her ear the broad effulgent blade
Sweeps down impetuous on the *meek one*'s head,
Loud rings the helmet to the rushing stroke,
Back flies the weapon into pieces broke;
The trusty metal's well-concocted frame
From heaven's own stores invulnerable came:
No human force the jointed plates could rend,
But darts recoil, and blows in vain descend.
No limits now the *monster*'s rage restrain,
To see her hand a *bladeless* hilt retain;
Stung with remorse, she tosses it aside,
A shameful monument of *fallen pride*;
Nor longer meditates the martial strife,
But seeks for means to end her wretched life;
Selects a javelin, on the sandy plain,
From heaps of others lately cast in vain;
