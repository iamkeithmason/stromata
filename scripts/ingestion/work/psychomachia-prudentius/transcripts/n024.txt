Why did they not exert an *earlier* claim,
Lost to all worth, to honour, and to fame?
But vain *tradition* still allures them on
With hope of crowns, that may some time be won;
This distant *hope* alleviates *present pain*,
And is the trick by which their *leaders* reign;
Fit bait for minds, whom glory never charms,
Nor sprightly trumpets animate to arms;
Cold, frozen souls, whom valour never fires,
Whose luke-warm temper nothing great desires;
What feats will CHASTITY's cold nerves perform?
In war will PIETY support a storm?
'Twixt shame and grief how wretchedly I'm tost,
To see myself oppos'd by such an host;
To see a band of *milk-fac'd maids* advance,
Not bred to combat, nor to poise the lance!
There JUSTICE, doom'd to indigence by fate;
And HONESTY, who never will be great;
SOBRIETY, just wither'd to a shade;
And pale-fac'd ABSTINENCE, a pining maid;
