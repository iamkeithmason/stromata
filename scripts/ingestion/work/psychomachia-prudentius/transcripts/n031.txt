Quick shoots the venom thro' each nerve, and vein,
And *Virtue* perishes, by *roses* slain.
A death-like stupor the whole host o'erspreads,
They drop their weapons, and they hang their heads,
As on the chariot's radiant frame they gaze,
And their eyes dazzle in the diamond blaze;
Or while the golden traces they behold,
And whirling axletree of solid gold,
Or silver spokes, that emulate the sun,
And in an orb of lucid amber run.
From rank to rank seditious murmurs fly,
For quick desertion is the gen'ral cry;
All long to serve the *ostentatious fool*,
And live with LUXURY, unbound by rule.
The *Queen* of *Temperance* severely mourn'd,
To see her *sons* to base *deserters* turn'd,
Her firm allies, who mighty shocks had stood,
Strike to mere *shew*, and perish without *blood*.
The *crosse's banner*, which the *princely maid*
Bore in the van, aloft in air display'd,
