Ev'n the *low vulgar* were allow'd to please,
Themselves in all the liberties of ease;
Safe in the camp each moment to employ
To heal past sorrows with succeeding joy.
Next, on a central eminence they place
A *double* pile, but on one *common* base;
The lower part, a fair tribunal, shews
August and spacious; but above it grows,
A narrower edifice; whose top commands
A boundless prospect over seas and lands.
Thither the *Queens* ascending take their seat,
With equal dignity, and equal state,
CONCORD and FAITH; precedence neither take,
But scorn all difference for agreement's sake.
Then thro' the camp their *messengers* they send,
And summon all their people to attend.
Chearful each *mental faculty* obeys
The sov'reign mandate, nor absconding stays;
Tho' numerous labyrinths the *mind* supply'd,
The ling'ring truants in its folds to hide:
