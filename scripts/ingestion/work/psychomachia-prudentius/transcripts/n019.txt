Then strikes th' inverted shaft of polish'd wood
Deep in the earth; the point erected stood,
Enters her breast, and drinks her vital blood.
Streight tow'rds the *haughty vanquish'd* PATIENCE came,
And mildly thus her triumph did proclaim;
At length victorious; *thus* our wars we wage,
Without th' *inhuman* marks of *bloody* rage;
From discipline, like this, we never part,
For *hardy suff'rance* is our *martial art*:
Unmov'd we stand, and let the *blast* go by,
For *Passion* always, like a *blast*, will die;
Her fretting spirits into *vapours* fume,
Or end, like flames, that will themselves consume.
She ceas'd; then shot, unwounded, thro' the throng,
Nobly attended as she pass'd along;
For JOB, still faithful, from his *sovereign*'s side
No force could seperate, nor threats divide;
But close he follow'd where she march'd before,
His face displaying all the wounds he bore;
