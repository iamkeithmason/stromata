Where'er the fiend her baleful influence sheds,
Crimes spring apace, and murd'rous rapine spreads;
Like rav'ning Wolves, the terror of the wood,
They roar for plunder, and they feast on blood.
A starry helmet lo! yon Soldier wears,
That starry helmet soon his life ensnares;
Observe his *Comrade* seeks a cause of strife,
And takes the jewels with the owner's life.
A belt, with gems adorn'd, has *there* supprest
The voice of nature in the filial breast;
Has drawn the *son*, for sordid hopes of gain,
To spoil the *parent*, by another slain:
The lust of lucre shoots the flaming brand
Of civil discord thro' a peaceful land,
To dire extremes th' insatiate rage has run;
The rav'ning *parent* robs his *only son*.
Such scenes of cruelty the *Monster* wrought,
And mighty numbers to destruction brought;
Ten thousand ways ten thousand ills contrives,
To rob deluded mortals of their lives;
