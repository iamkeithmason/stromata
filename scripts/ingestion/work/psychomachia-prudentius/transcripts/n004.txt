That barb'rous Conquerors constrain
His Nephew in a servile Chain.
Breathing Revenge he strait reviews
His *menial* Servants, and pursues
The flying Victors, whom a Load
Of Spoil encumber'd on the Road.
*Old Age* itself, with Sword in Hand,
Leads on the firm *domestick Band*;
And full of Energy divine,
In pious Arms delights to shine.
Th' o'erloaded, straggling Rout of Foes
He beats, disperses, overthrows;
Redeems the Captives from their Chains,
And plunder'd SODOM's Spoil regains;
*Soft Virgins*, whom in Bonds they bore;
And *rural Wealth*, a countless Store;
Much *Gold*, and costly *Suits* of *Dress*,
And *houshold Treasures* numberless.
Then LOT himself, who groaning bow'd
Beneath the galling Fetter's Load,
