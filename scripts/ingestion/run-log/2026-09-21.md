**Stage 1 — copyright.** Author d. c. 413. The translation is anonymous and was

## Read before publishing

`scripts/ingestion/output/psychomachia-prudentius-review.md`. It lists:

- **59 places where the scan appears to have lost a line.** Leaded verse has a
  near-constant line pitch, so a wider gap means type that did not come through.
  The couplets either side of the ones I spot-checked do not rhyme, so these are
  real losses, not detector noise. They need checking against the page images.
- **423 lines the OCR itself flagged as low confidence**, left uncorrected on
  purpose — guessing an 18th-century word from context is how an edition acquires
  readings no printed copy ever had.
- Every line dropped as page furniture, with its reason, and every stray mark
  removed from inside a line.

`ocr_quality` is set to `low` deliberately. The arrangement is sound — every line
is a line, in order, furniture gone. The letters inside them come from a British
Library microfilm scan and are not.
