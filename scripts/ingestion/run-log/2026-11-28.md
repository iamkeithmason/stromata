## Details

### the-great-didactic-didactica-magna-comenius — The Great Didactic (Comenius)
Before this run: the collection `great-didactic` had been live since May 2026 with the front matter (Greeting,
Dedicatory Letter, The Use of the Art of Teaching) and Chapters I–II. The September prospect pointed at the
Wikisource title page and a fresh collection slug, so running it as queued would have fetched one near-empty
page into a duplicate collection. Repointed to `great-didactic` and the 31 chapter pages.

Stage 1: Comenius d. 1670. Keatinge d. 1934, but the translation was published 1896: public domain in the US by
publication date and in the UK since 2005. Import clears on the author's death year. All 31 chapter URLs 200.

Stage 2: `fetch-texts.mjs` (source `wikisource`) on
`…/The_Great_Didactic_of_John_Amos_Comenius/The_Great_Didactic/Chapter_3` to `Chapter_33`. Cleanup on the
staged files:
- Titles and slugs from each chapter's own heading ("Chapter III: This Life is but a Preparation for Eternity"),
  matching the live Chapters I–II; slugs capped at ten words. `collection_order` 6–36.
- Removed the `CHAPTER N` / title lines (the reader shows the title), the fetcher's editorial-intro comment, and
  MediaWiki's `Retrieved from "…oldid=…"` footer (now stripped at source, see CHANGELOG).
- Removed 28 Wikisource link numbers to Keatinge's endnotes (15–42, e.g. "Vives15"). The endnotes themselves
  are not imported, matching the live chapters.
- Rewrote two layout tables that came through as scattered fragments (XXII: the four ages of language study;
  XXVII: the four schools) and XXX's history list as plain lines; fixed the "(iv)(i)" list-label artefact.
- Corrected plain transcription slips in the Wikisource text where the sense is unambiguous: "yea And" →
  "years. And" (III), "ofeducation", "increasedas" (VIII), "areconducted" (XXXIII), "sinople"/"ther" (XXII),
  "causation Finally" (XXVII), and about twenty single-word slips the note-draft agents found (e.g. "the tea
  himself" → "the teacher himself" XIX, "spa of life" → "span" XIV, "worship of Go" XXIV). Recorded in each
  text's `provenance_notes`.
- Section numbers ("1\.") kept as printed. The live Chapters I–II were imported without them.

Checked against the 1896 printing (two independent Archive.org OCRs, `greatdidacticofj00come` and
`greatdidacticjo00keatgoog`), after the import: every correction above matches the print, and seven more readings
were restored to it in the staged files and the draft rows: the Isidore quotation (XXV §17), "(with the exception
of monstrosities)" (XXVI), "(making allowance for the holidays)" (XXXII), "the type are inked, that a distinct
impression" (XXXII §23), "rivulets flow … will say more in the following" (XVIII §40), "opposition" (XXXIII) and
"(iii) By seizing opportunities" (XIV §14).

Printed slips, settled with Keith the same morning: "sound" corrected to "taste" (V §6) and a missing full stop
added (XIX), both silently. Bracketed editor's notes, `[Ed.: …]`, added where the print is wrong or obscure but
left standing: "(Rev. xi. 1)" (III §4, probably Rev. xx. 11), "chap. xxxi" (XXIII, discipline is in XXVI),
"2945 hours" (XV §16), "vulgary" (XVIII §25, a vernacular phrase-book).

Chapters I and II (published since May, without section numbers): bodies re-taken from Wikisource with the
numbered sections, footnote markers removed, and a note appended to `provenance_notes`. Edited by SQL, so their
pages refresh on Keith's next save in the admin form or within the week's cache window.
