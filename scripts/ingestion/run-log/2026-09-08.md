  to the Corinthian church on factionalism; one of the earliest non-canonical Christian documents.
- **Epistle of Polycarp to the Philippians** (Polycarp of Smyrna, d. c. 155) — references Ignatius
  directly; Notion suggests pairing with the Martyrdom as a two-text collection.
- **The Martyrdom of Polycarp** (Anonymous, Church of Smyrna) — earliest extant Christian
  martyrdom account (c. 155–167). No author death year; clearance rests on the ANF translation
  (Roberts d. 1901), same basis already used for `epistle-to-diognetus`.
- **All of Grace** (C.H. Spurgeon, d. 1892) — his most widely read short evangelistic treatise
  (1886); original English, copyright clear outright on the author's death year.
- **The Cloud of Unknowing** (Anonymous, 14th c.; ed. Evelyn Underhill, 1922) — Notion's
  editorial notes cite UK life+70 as the copyright basis, but the operative rule here is US
  publication date: 1922 predates 1929, so it's unconditionally public domain regardless of
  Underhill's 1941 death year. Recorded explicitly per CLAUDE.md's publication-date-vs-
  translator-death-year guidance. Do not substitute the Progoff (1957) or Wolters/Penguin (1961)
  translations — both still in copyright.

Full properties, reasoning, and reference URLs recorded in each prospect's `notes` field in
