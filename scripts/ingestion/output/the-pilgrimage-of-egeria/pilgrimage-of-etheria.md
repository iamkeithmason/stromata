## Summary

![Read this book](https://ccel.org/ccel/mcclure/etheria/files/etheria-thumb.jpg "Read this book")

Read this work

_The Pilgrimage of Etheria_ is a firsthand account one woman's trip to the Holy Land. Based on the contents of the book, one can assume the woman making the pilgrimage was well educated and knew her Bible. Etheria is strong in personality and motivation to achieve her goals. As she details how people in Zion worship, the work becomes a valuable window into the religious practices of that day. This work was written by Mary Louisa McClure.  
  
Wailand Groenendyk
