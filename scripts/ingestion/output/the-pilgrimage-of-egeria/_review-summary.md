## Collection description

```
Egeria’s account of her pilgrimage to the holy sites of the Eastern Roman Empire in the late 4th century is one of the most precious and vivid records we possess of early Christian life and devotion. Her detailed descriptions of worship in Jerusalem, Bethlehem, and the monastic communities of the East open a window into the liturgical and devotional practices of the Jerusalem Church, and her reflections on prayer, Scripture, and the presence of God offer timeless spiritual wisdom. This volume presents a modern English translation of her travelogue, allowing readers to walk alongside Egeria as she journeys through the Holy Land, experiencing its sacred places and participating in its liturgical life.
```
