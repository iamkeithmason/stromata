note_draft: "It never mentions its own name: 'quick and the dead,' the descent into hell, the communion of saints — phrases so worn into English devotion that most who say them weekly could not date a single clause. Yet every line answers a controversy someone was prepared to die over: 'born of the Virgin Mary' against those who denied Christ came in the flesh at all, 'the resurrection of the body' against those who found a body beneath a Christian's dignity. It is the shortest and oldest of the church's three ecumenical creeds, built for a candidate to speak aloud at the font rather than for a council to argue over. Read it as a rope let down through eighteen centuries, still gripped at both ends."

I believe in God the Father Almighty, Maker of heaven and earth.

And in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Ghost, born of the Virgin Mary; suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, dead, and buried; He descended into hell; the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven, and sitteth on the right hand of God the Father Almighty; from thence He shall come to judge the quick and the dead.

I believe in the Holy Ghost; the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints; the forgiveness of sins; the resurrection of the body; and the life everlasting. Amen.
